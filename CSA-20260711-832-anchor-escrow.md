## Executive Summary
The Anchor Escrow smart contract contains a critical access control vulnerability in the `cancel()` instruction. The function lacks verification that the caller is the initializer, allowing any account to cancel any active escrow and potentially steal funds. This finding must be fixed immediately. Once the proposed constraint is added, the contract will be safe under standard assumptions. The overall verdict is CONDITIONAL, meaning the contract is not safe in its current state.

## Key Findings
- **V-01 Missing Cancel Authority Check**: The `cancel()` function does not enforce that `initializer` (the signer) matches `escrow_state.initializer_key`. Without this check, a malicious actor can invoke cancel on any escrow, reclaiming the tokens held. This is a direct loss-of-funds vulnerability.
